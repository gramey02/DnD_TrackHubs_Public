D&D Gene Editing Targets

This track displays disease mutation-agnostic and allele-specific CRISPR editing sites for dominant and dispensable (D&D) genes.

Each feature on the track represents a labeled single nucleotide polymorphism (SNP), where only SNPs with a minor allele frequency (MAF) above 0.1 are shown. Additionally, only biallelic SNPs are shown, with a few minor exceptions (some multiallelic SNPs with an extremely rare third allele are also shown). MAFs and SNPs were taken from the 1000 Genomes Phase 3 high coverage data (N=2504).

Each SNP is targetable by at least one of four gene editing strategies. Use the "Filter by gene editing strategy" control to show only SNPs matching one or more of the select editing approaches.

Color legend

Color Description
  MAF < 0.3 and targetable by at least one strategy out of exon disruption, epigenetic silencing, or splice site disruption. Note that these SNPs may also be targetable by excision.
  MAF >= 0.5 and targetable by at least one strategy out of exon disruption, epigenetic silencing, or splice site disruption. Note that these SNPs may also be targetable by excision.
  Any MAF, but ONLY targetable by excision.
